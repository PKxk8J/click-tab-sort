You can sort tabs from the right click menu on tabs. Sort orders are
  • URL
  • Title
  • Opened date
  • Accessed date
  • Random
  • Reverse order
* Opened dates are reset when Firefox starts. Pinned tabs are sorted when the right click menu is opened on pinned tabs. Tab groups are sorted as blocks at the top level, and tabs inside each group can be sorted separately. Split view tabs keep their internal order and move as one block. Containers are ignored when sorting. Optional notifications show progress.
